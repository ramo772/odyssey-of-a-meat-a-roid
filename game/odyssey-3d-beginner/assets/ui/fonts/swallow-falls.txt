Swallow Falls Regular, by Zack VerVynck.
The zip from dafont (swallow_falls) contained Swallow Falls.ttf and Swallow Falls Italic.ttf and no readme.
FontZillion, FontGet, and BlogFonts list this face as free for personal and commercial use, with redistribution allowed.
This game embeds the regular file for Latin display lettering only.
Arabic uses Cairo and Lalezar under the SIL Open Font License (see OFL.txt).
Numbers, equations, and small reading text use Cairo so they stay readable.
